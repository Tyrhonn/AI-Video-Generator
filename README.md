# An Integrated AI Pipeline for Historical Character Talking-Head Generation in Digital Museums and History Education

**Official implementation of our research on an integrated AI pipeline for generating historical-character talking-head videos from portrait images and textual or prerecorded speech.**

---

## Introduction

Generative AI has enabled the synthesis of audio-driven talking-head videos from portrait images, creating new possibilities for digital education and cultural heritage applications. In particular, historical portraits can be transformed into talking-head representations that serve as virtual narrators for educational content and interactive exhibitions. However, generating such videos requires the integration of multiple processing stages, including portrait background removal, Vietnamese text-to-speech synthesis, audio-driven facial animation, and video post-processing.

This project implements an integrated Web-based AI pipeline for generating historical-character talking-head videos from portrait images and either textual scripts or prerecorded speech. The system combines Remove.bg for portrait preprocessing, VieNeu-TTS for Vietnamese speech synthesis, and SadTalker for audio-driven facial animation, followed by video post-processing to produce the final output. Rather than introducing a new generative model, the project focuses on evaluating, selecting, and integrating existing AI technologies into a practical workflow.

The resulting videos are intended for offline playback in digital museums, interactive exhibitions, and history education. The system also supports deployment on visualization platforms such as HoloFan, providing a physical demonstration of how the generated content can be incorporated into exhibition environments.

# Pipeline

The proposed pipeline consists of five sequential stages:

```text
   Text Input                   Portrait Image
       │                              │
       ▼                              ▼
      TTS                    Background Removal
   (VieNeu-TTS)                    (Remove.bg)
       │                              │
       ▼                              ▼
 Speech (.wav)             Foreground Image (.png)
       │                              │
       └──────────────┐┌──────────────┘
                      ▼▼
            Talking Head Generation
                  (SadTalker)
                      │
                      ▼
               Face Enhancement
                   (GFPGAN)
                      │
                      ▼
             HoloFan Ready Video
```
